About GamePlan

GamePlan started because too much of a coach’s work was living in different places.

The idea came from a problem I kept running into in my own work: one part of the thinking lived in the game model, another in the weekly plan, another in training notes and another in the match review. Coming back to it later meant rebuilding the context.

My relationship with football began with my grandfather, Adão, an amateur coach. He taught me early to look at the game through intelligence, cooperation and purpose. I started coaching in the United States in 2017 and have since worked across different clubs, countries and football cultures before arriving in the Netherlands.

The same question kept coming back: did what we worked on during the week actually show up in the match? And if it didn’t, where should that observation live so it can shape the next week? That need is where GamePlan started to take shape.

GamePlan brings the game model, exercises, planning, players and match analysis into one environment. It gives the process structure without choosing the methodology or making the technical decision. That stays with the coach.
