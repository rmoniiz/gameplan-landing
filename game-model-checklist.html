Game Model Building Checklist

This material does not tell you what football to play. Use it to review whether your own idea is clear, observable and connected to training and the match.

10 points5–10 minutesNo imposed methodology
01

Core idea

Can you explain in a few sentences what your team is trying to do and what defines your game most clearly?

02

With the ball

Is it clear how the team progresses, occupies space, creates advantages and reaches finishing areas?

03

Without the ball

Have you defined how the team protects space, presses, guides the opponent and reorganizes?

04

After regaining possession

Do players know what to notice and decide in the first seconds after winning the ball?

05

After losing possession

Is there a clear intention for reacting to loss and reorganizing when immediate pressure is not possible?

06

Observable behaviours

Are your principles translated into actions that can be recognized, described and discussed?

07

Decision and perception

For each important behaviour, is it clear what the player needs to perceive before deciding?

08

Connection to training

Do exercises create situations where these behaviours and decisions can appear?

09

Match evidence

Have you defined which signals help you recognize whether training appeared on the pitch?

10

Reflection and next cycle

After the match, can you connect evidence to next week’s priorities without rebuilding the reasoning from scratch?
