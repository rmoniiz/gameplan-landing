Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

This Policy explains in clear language how GamePlan processes personal data when you visit the landing page, create an account, use the platform, contact support or subscribe to a plan.

1. Data controller

GamePlan is currently operated by Renan Moniz in the Netherlands. For privacy, support or data-rights requests, contact vmonizperformance@gmail.com.

The final registered business name, registration number and business address will be added before commercial launch. This version remains subject to pre-launch legal review.

2. Data we process

  • Account and authentication data, including name, email, user identifier, language and session information.
  • Profile and configuration data, such as coach name, club, logo and interface preferences.
  • Coach-created content, including game models, exercises, plans, player records, evaluations, tactical tests, match statistics and notes.
  • Technical and usage data, including visited pages, product events, device, browser, IP address, error logs and security information.
  • Commercial and subscription data, including plan, status, dates, customer, transaction and invoice identifiers. GamePlan does not store full card numbers.
  • Messages sent to support or through contact channels.

3. Purposes

  • Create, authenticate and protect accounts.
  • Store, synchronize, retrieve and display coaching work.
  • Connect game model, exercises, planning, players, match and reflection.
  • Manage trials, plans, subscriptions, cancellations and module access.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access and technical failures.
  • Measure usage, stability and user experience to improve the product.
  • Answer support, account and legal requests.

4. Legal bases

Depending on the context, processing may be necessary to perform a contract and provide the service, comply with legal obligations, pursue legitimate interests in security and product improvement, or rely on consent where appropriate. Consent may be withdrawn at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing.

5. Providers and sharing

We share only what is needed with providers supporting the service:

  • Supabase: authentication, database, storage and backend functions.
  • Vercel: hosting, delivery and observability.
  • Paddle: checkout, billing, payments, tax and transactional support when paid subscriptions are active. Paddle may act as Merchant of Record or authorized reseller.
  • PostHog: product analytics, navigation events and experience diagnostics.

We may also disclose information where required by law, a competent authority, the protection of rights or a legitimate reorganization of the service.

6. International transfers

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where required, recognized safeguards are used, such as adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses or other valid mechanisms.

7. Analytics, cookies and local storage

The landing page and app may use cookies, local storage and similar technologies for authentication, language, preferences, security and analytics. Strictly necessary technologies may operate without additional consent; optional technologies will be handled according to applicable law and the choices made available to users.

8. Player and minor data

The coach decides which player data to record and is responsible for an appropriate legal basis and required permissions, especially for minors. Avoid medical, biometric or other sensitive data unless genuinely necessary. GamePlan does not replace the club’s or coach’s duties toward players and guardians.

9. Retention and deletion

Data is kept for as long as needed to provide the service, secure accounts and comply with legal, tax, accounting or security obligations. After account closure, limited copies may remain temporarily in backups or mandatory records. Deletion requests may be sent to the contact in this Policy.

10. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request information, access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection, withdraw consent and complain to a competent data-protection authority. We may verify identity before processing a request.

11. Security and automated decisions

We use proportionate technical and organizational controls, including authentication, database authorization, event logs and secret protection. No system is risk-free. AI-assisted insights are suggestions for coach review and do not independently produce legal or similarly significant decisions about players or users.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this Policy to reflect changes in the product, providers or law. Material updates will be communicated appropriately. Contact: vmonizperformance@gmail.com.
