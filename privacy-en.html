Privacy Policy

Version: 2026-08-24

This Policy explains which data GamePlan processes, why it is needed, which providers may process it and how users can exercise their rights.

The product architecture isolates private coach content by account and uses Supabase as the persistent source for functional data.

1. Responsible operator and contact

Current operation: GamePlan, operational owner Renan Moniz, Netherlands. Privacy and support contact: vmonizperformance@gmail.com.

Formal business identification will be updated before commercial launch. This is a pre-commercial operational policy and should receive professional legal review before commercial activation.

2. Data we process

  • Account and authentication: email, user identifier, language and technical data needed for login and security.
  • Profile and access: role, plan, trial dates, subscription state and legal-acceptance versions/timestamps.
  • Coach-created content: game model, principles, behaviours, exercises, planning, players, assessments, attendance, matches, events, statistics, reports and important drafts.
  • Player data: only what the coach chooses to record for the sporting purpose. The coach must have appropriate authority, particularly for minors.
  • Technical and security data: logs and information needed for availability, abuse prevention, diagnosis and recovery.
  • Landing analytics: only after optional visitor consent.

3. Purposes

  • create, authenticate and protect the account;
  • save, synchronize, organize and display the coach's work;
  • apply trial, permission and plan rules correctly;
  • prevent cross-account access and investigate incidents;
  • handle support and data-rights requests;
  • measure landing use only when analytics is accepted;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Legal bases and choices

Processing necessary to provide and protect the service is carried out in connection with the service relationship and legitimate security/operational interests where applicable. Legal obligations may require specific processing. Non-essential landing analytics is based on consent and can be refused or withdrawn without preventing use of the site.

5. Service providers

GamePlan uses specialized providers to deliver infrastructure. This currently includes Supabase for authentication, database and backend; Vercel for hosting and Previews; and PostHog in the European region for optional analytics when accepted. Payment providers process data only when approved billing is activated.

GamePlan does not need to store full payment-card numbers. Each provider also operates under its own terms, subprocessors and security controls.

6. Analytics, cookies and local storage

The landing page uses essential storage to remember preferences, including the analytics choice. The PostHog SDK is not loaded before affirmative consent. When accepted, the landing configuration limits collection to GamePlan-defined events, disables autocapture and session recording on this page, and uses PostHog's European endpoint.

The choice can be changed at any time through Privacy preferences in the landing footer.

7. Security and isolation

Functional data is protected with authentication, Row Level Security (RLS), owner boundaries and separation between client and privileged service paths. Privileged keys must not be exposed to the browser. No security mechanism removes all risk; incidents are handled through a dedicated operational process.

8. Retention

Functional data is retained while needed for the account and service unless a valid deletion request or legal retention duty applies. Consented analytics follows the provider project's configured retention. Technical logs may be retained as needed for security, diagnosis and applicable obligations.

9. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and withdraw consent when processing depends on it. GamePlan may request reasonable identity confirmation before performing requests that affect account data.

10. Export, correction and deletion

The app provides a Privacy Center to export functional data accessible to the account in JSON format without including tokens or credentials. Editable content can be corrected in the relevant modules. Requests concerning account data or permanent deletion are sent to support and handled through a controlled process to reduce unintended irreversible loss.

11. Minors' data

GamePlan is a tool for coaches. A coach or organization entering player data must verify its authority and local obligations, especially for minors. We recommend recording only information needed for the sporting purpose and avoiding sensitive information unless it is genuinely necessary.

12. Location and transfers

The main GamePlan database is currently hosted in Supabase's European region. Other providers may operate infrastructure or subprocessors in other jurisdictions, subject to the safeguards applicable to their services.

13. Changes and contact

Material changes may create a new Policy version and, where required, renewed acceptance in the app. To exercise rights, raise a concern or request support: vmonizperformance@gmail.com.
