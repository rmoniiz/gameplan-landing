Privacy Policy

Version: 2026-09-04

This Policy explains which data GamePlan processes, why it is needed, which providers may process it and how users can exercise their rights.

Private coach content is associated with the relevant account and protected by the service's access controls.

1. Responsible operator and contact

GamePlan is operated by Renan Moniz in the Netherlands. Privacy and support contact: vmonizperformance@gmail.com.

2. Data we process

  • Account and authentication: email, user identifier, language and technical data needed for login and security.
  • Profile and access: role, plan, trial dates, subscription state and legal-acceptance versions/timestamps.
  • Coach-created content: game model, principles, behaviours, exercises, planning, players, assessments, attendance, matches, events, statistics, reports and important drafts.
  • Player data: only what the coach chooses to record for the sporting purpose. The coach must have appropriate authority, particularly for minors.
  • Free-resource requests: name, email, language, request source and, when present in the link used, campaign parameters, together with the version and timestamp of the consent given to store those details and release the requested resource.
  • Technical and security data: logs and information needed for availability, abuse prevention, diagnosis and recovery.
  • Optional analytics: GamePlan-defined usage events on the landing page and authenticated app, only after affirmative consent on the relevant surface. Collection is designed not to include player names, coach-created work text, observations, notes, or account email as analytics properties. Names and emails submitted to request free resources are also not sent as analytics properties.

3. Purposes

  • create, authenticate and protect the account;
  • save, synchronize, organize and display the coach's work;
  • apply trial, permission and plan rules correctly;
  • prevent cross-account access and investigate incidents;
  • deliver requested free resources, record the request language and source, and keep the related consent record;
  • handle support and data-rights requests;
  • measure landing and product use only when optional analytics is accepted;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Legal bases and choices

Processing necessary to provide and protect the service is carried out in connection with the service relationship and legitimate security and operational interests where applicable. Legal obligations may require specific processing. Non-essential analytics is based on consent and can be refused or withdrawn without preventing use of essential website or app functionality.

When you request a free resource, GamePlan asks for specific consent to store the details submitted in the form and release that resource. This consent does not automatically authorize promotional campaigns or marketing emails. It can be withdrawn by contacting the address provided in this Policy.

5. Service providers

GamePlan uses specialized providers to operate the service, including Supabase for authentication and data storage, Vercel for hosting and PostHog in the European region for optional analytics when accepted.

When a transaction takes place, the applicable payment provider may process the information required for payment. GamePlan does not store full payment-card numbers. Each provider operates under its own terms, subprocessors and security controls.

6. Analytics, cookies and local storage

The landing page uses essential storage to remember preferences, including the analytics choice. Optional analytics is activated only after affirmative consent and can be refused or withdrawn at any time.

When analytics is active, GamePlan limits collection to defined product-usage events and does not use session recording on this landing page. In the authenticated app, the analytics preference is associated with the account so it can follow the user across devices. The analytics project is also configured to anonymize IP addresses.

The landing choice can be changed through Privacy preferences in the site footer. The app choice can be changed in the Privacy Center. Refusing or withdrawing analytics does not block essential functionality.

7. Security and isolation

Functional data is protected through authentication, account-level access controls and separated permissions. Details submitted through the free-resource form are written through a server endpoint and are not available for direct reading or editing by landing-page visitors. GamePlan uses technical and operational measures to reduce unauthorized access and protect private content. No security mechanism removes all risk, so incidents are handled through a dedicated operational process.

8. Retention

Functional data is retained while needed for the account and service unless a valid deletion request or legal retention duty applies. Details submitted to request a free resource are retained while needed to record and fulfil the request and can be subject to a valid deletion request or consent withdrawal, unless an applicable legal duty requires otherwise. Consented analytics follows the provider project's configured retention. Technical logs may be retained as needed for security, diagnosis and applicable obligations.

9. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and withdraw consent when processing depends on it. GamePlan may request reasonable identity confirmation before performing requests that affect account data or contact details submitted through forms.

10. Export, correction and deletion

The app provides a Privacy Center to export functional data accessible to the account in JSON format without including tokens or credentials. Editable content can be corrected in the relevant modules. Requests concerning account data, details submitted through forms or permanent deletion are sent to support and handled through a controlled process to reduce unintended irreversible loss.

11. Minors' data

GamePlan is a tool for coaches. A coach or organization entering player data must verify its authority and local obligations, especially for minors. We recommend recording only information needed for the sporting purpose and avoiding sensitive information unless it is genuinely necessary.

12. Location and transfers

The main GamePlan database is currently hosted in Supabase's European region. Other providers may operate infrastructure or subprocessors in other jurisdictions, subject to the safeguards applicable to their services.

13. Changes and contact

Material changes may create a new Policy version and, where required, renewed acceptance in the app. To exercise rights, withdraw consent related to a free-resource request, raise a concern or request support: vmonizperformance@gmail.com.
